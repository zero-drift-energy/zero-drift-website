The Problem

Your broker is working for the supplier.

Most UK energy brokers earn hidden commissions from the suppliers they recommend. Those commissions come directly from your unit rate — sometimes over £700 a year — with no legal obligation to disclose them.

That's not brokerage. It's a conflict of interest dressed up as advice.
